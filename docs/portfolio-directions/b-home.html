Inspect this system ↗

One grammar rule. One function.

A recursive descent parser makes the language structure visible in the implementation. The cost is a parser coupled to a fixed grammar.

Why this boundary matters

Precedence follows from the nesting of expression, term and factor. Errors terminate through one match function, keeping diagnostics precise at the cost of reporting one error per run.
