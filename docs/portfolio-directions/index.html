My recommendation: A

Working journal gives technical material room to breathe while remaining approachable to hiring managers. Its margin can carry role, constraints and context without interrupting the main account.

The strongest alternative: B

Choose Systems atlas if the architecture should be the first thing people see. It becomes stronger as the professional case studies gain confirmed diagrams. A can borrow its semantic diagram conventions without adopting the whole layout.
